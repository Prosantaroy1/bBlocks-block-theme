Seller questions

Answers Before You Decide

Selling a home is a big step. Here is what owners ask us most. Still unsure? A real person will happily walk you through it.

Ask Us Anything
How do you decide on my offer?

We look at recent sales of similar homes nearby, the repairs the house needs and current market conditions. We share that math with you so the offer is never a mystery.

Are there any fees or commissions?

No. There are no agent commissions and no service fees. In most sales we also pay the standard closing costs, so the offer you accept is the amount you receive.

Do I need to clean or fix anything first?

Not at all. Leave the repairs, the clutter and any items you do not want. We buy the house exactly as it stands today.

How fast can we close?

Many sales close in about two weeks once the title is clear. If you need more time to plan a move, we can set a later date that works for you.

Am I obligated to accept the offer?

Never. Our offer is free and comes with no strings attached. Take your time, ask questions and compare it with other options before you decide.
