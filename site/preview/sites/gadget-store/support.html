Quick answers about shipping, warranty, returns and payment. Still unsure? Our team is one message away.

How long does shipping take?

In-stock orders placed before 3 pm ship the same day. Standard delivery takes 1–3 business days and is free on orders over $49; express next-day delivery is available at checkout.

What does the 2-year warranty cover?

Our warranty covers manufacturing faults and hardware failures for two years from delivery, including parts and labour. Accidental damage is not covered, but you can add protection at checkout.

How do I return a product?

Start a return from your account within 30 days of delivery and print the free label. Once we receive the item in its original condition, we refund you within five business days.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards, digital wallets and bank transfer. Every payment is encrypted and processed securely; we never store your full card details.

Can I get help choosing the right device?

Of course. Our product specialists are available by chat, phone and email seven days a week and will happily compare models for you.
