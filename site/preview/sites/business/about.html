Our story starts over twenty years ago in a cramped co-working space downtown.

"Working with this team let me build the kind of experiences I always imagined, without writing a line of code along the way. It's truly inspiring."

Michelle Harris, Astrocode

We were founded by a small group of designers frustrated with the gap between visual creativity and technical implementation. What began with three people and a shared desk has grown into a team of makers who help brands launch better websites, faster.

Our breakthrough came when we turned our own design system into a product. Teams could finally build fully working pages without waiting on a developer, and word spread quickly among agencies and growing companies.

Looking ahead, we keep investing in tools that remove busywork while staying true to one belief: creativity should never be held back by technical limits. Our community keeps pushing us to make that real.
