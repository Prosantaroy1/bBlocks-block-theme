Questions patients often ask

Do I need a referral to see a specialist?

No. You can book most specialists directly. If your insurance plan requires a referral, our front desk will arrange it with your family doctor.

Which insurance plans do you accept?

We work with most major health and dental insurers. Call us or send a message with your plan details and we will confirm your cover before your visit.

How quickly can I get an appointment?

Most patients are seen within the same week, and we keep slots free every day for urgent visits.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card, a list of current medicines and any recent test results or reports.

Can I get my lab results online?

Yes. Results are shared securely through our patient portal, and your doctor will call you if anything needs a follow-up.
