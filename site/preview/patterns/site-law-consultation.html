Frequently asked questions

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. Your first 30-minute meeting is free and confidential, with no obligation to hire us afterwards.

How much will my case cost?

It depends on the matter. We offer flat fees, hourly rates and contingency arrangements, and always explain costs in writing before any work begins.

What should I bring to our first meeting?

Bring any letters, contracts, court papers or notes related to your situation, plus a photo ID. If you are unsure, bring it anyway.

How long will my case take?

Some matters resolve in weeks while others take longer. We give you a realistic timeline at the start and keep you updated at every stage.

Do you offer virtual meetings?

Yes. You can meet your attorney in our office, by phone or by secure video call, whichever suits you best.
