Homes in Every Corner of Millbrook

Our buildings sit in three friendly neighborhoods, each with its own pace and plenty of local favorites nearby.

Riverside

Our oldest streets, right on the water. Walk to cafés, the boat launch and the summer concerts in the park.

Old Mill District

The busy heart of Millbrook, with upstairs apartments, Main Street shops and offices above the market square.

North Hill

Quiet, leafy blocks of garden apartments and family homes close to schools, trails and the community pool.
