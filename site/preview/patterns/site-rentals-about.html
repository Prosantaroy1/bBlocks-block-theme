A Family-Owned Rental Company Rooted in Millbrook

Harbor Lane Rentals began with one riverside duplex and a toolbox. Today our family owns and cares for apartments across Millbrook, a few rental houses near the park and a row of shop and office units downtown.

Because we live here too, we keep things personal: clear leases, quick repairs and people you can actually reach. Whether you need a furnished place for a season or a storefront for your new business, we will help you find the right fit.
